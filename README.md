## 🧭 About

I build practical digital products that connect **software, automation, AI, and information**.

My current work focuses on **Telegram automation, news aggregation, bot systems, web development, and AI-assisted workflows**. I enjoy taking a repetitive process, designing the system behind it, and turning it into something that runs reliably with minimal manual work.

Alongside development, I study **Finance and Banking** as a BBA student, which shapes how I think about products, data, business problems, and decision-making — I bring a product and business lens to engineering, not just a technical one.

> **Build useful things. Automate the repetitive. Keep learning.**

**Open to:** Internships · Freelance projects · Open-source collaboration · Interesting problems to automate
